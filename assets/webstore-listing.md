## Detailed description

Reply Scout turns your X feed into a scored, draftable queue instead of an endless scroll.

It reads the posts currently on your screen, scores each one against a thesis and rubric you write yourself, and drafts a reply in your own voice for anything worth engaging. You edit, copy, and post by hand — it never posts, likes, or follows on its own.

No X API needed. It only sees what your logged-in browser already renders.

**How it works**
- Click "Scan visible posts" on your Home feed or any List. Up to 15 posts get scored per scan.
- Review the results, sorted by score. Edit any draft before you use it — you're always the last filter.
- Copy the reply, jump to the post, paste, and post it yourself.

**Morning digest (optional)**
A separate one-click feature that answers a different question: not "should I reply," but "what's actually worth reading right now." Scrolls your feed itself, then hands back a short list of what stood out and why.

**Your rules, not ours**
The thesis, rubric, digest focus, and voice samples are plain text you write and edit in settings — there's no fixed persona baked in. Reply Scout scores against your judgment, not a generic template.

**Claude or fully local**
Use the Anthropic API, or point it at your own LM Studio server and run everything locally — your choice.

**Privacy**
Your API key and settings live in Chrome's local extension storage. Post text (and, if you enable it, post images) is sent only to Anthropic's API or to your own local model server. Nothing is collected, logged, or sent anywhere else.

**You stay hand-on-the-wheel**
The copy-only design is deliberate. Automated posting from a browser session is against X's rules, and it would undercut the whole point — replies only work when they're genuinely yours.
